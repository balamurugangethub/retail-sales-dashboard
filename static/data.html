This public demo is read-only. You can try the cleaning preview, but importing and editing only work when you run the app on your own computer.

1. Bring in your sales data

CSV, TSV or Excel (.xlsx) from your till, POS or spreadsheet. Column names don't need to match: the app recognises things like "Branch", "Amount", "Qty" and fixes dates, currency symbols and spelling. Nothing is saved until you click Import.

Which column is which?

Change a choice if the guess is wrong, then click Re-check.
