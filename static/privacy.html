Retail Sales Dashboard

Privacy and cookie policy

Version 2026-10-11. If this policy changes, we ask you to accept it again.

Who we are

Retail Sales Dashboard helps shop owners and retail chains see their sales in one place. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it. Questions go to whoever runs this copy of the app.

What we store

  • Account details: your name, shop name, email address and the date you accepted this policy. Your password is stored only as a salted one-way hash, never as plain text.
  • Your business data: the sales files you import, the edits you make and the dashboards you save. Each account has its own separate database that no other account can see.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the AI provider described below.

Cookies

We only use cookies the app needs to work. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

CookieWhyHow long
rsd_consentRemembers that you accepted this policy.1 year
rsd_sessionKeeps you signed in. It is signed so it can't be forged.14 days, or until you sign out

On the public demo, dashboards you save are kept in your own browser's storage and never reach our server.

AI assistant

The assistant works without any outside service using built-in rules. If the person running the app has connected an AI provider (such as Google Gemini, Groq or a local Ollama), your question and a summary of your data (totals and the names of stores, categories and top products) are sent to that provider to write the answer. Their own privacy terms apply to that request.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and data until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, your sales data and your saved dashboards straight away.

Your choices

  • Decline: you can decline this policy on the welcome screen. Nothing is stored, but you can't use the app.
  • Download: export your sales data as a CSV file from your account page at any time.
  • Delete: delete your account and all of its data from your account page.
  • Change your mind: review your cookie choice.
